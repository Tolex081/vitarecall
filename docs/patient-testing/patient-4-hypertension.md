## Day 1 — start a supportive conversation

Send one prompt at a time and respond naturally using only the supplied fictional facts. If Vita asks for a missing medication or lab detail, say it has not been supplied in the test.

1. “Hello Vita. This is a fictional patient test. I'm Grace, 54. My clinician previously diagnosed high blood pressure. I feel well today and want help preparing for follow-up, not a new diagnosis. Please help without making me feel blamed.”
2. “I often eat rice, beans and vegetable soups. I find food labels confusing, and I want affordable examples of lower-salt choices. Could you ask what I normally cook before suggesting changes?”
3. “Please compare two realistic lunches, for example rice with beans and vegetables versus instant noodles with a salty seasoning packet. Explain how someone could adjust the ingredients, without claiming that one meal cures high blood pressure.”
4. “I have a home blood-pressure monitor. What should I ask a clinician or pharmacist about checking it, using it correctly and keeping a useful readings log?”
